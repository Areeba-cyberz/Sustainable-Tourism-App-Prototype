# Năm: Sustainable Tourism App Prototype (Vietnam)

An interactive app prototype for **Năm**, a sustainable tourism concept for Vietnam. It was created as a **team project** during a two-week cultural immersion programme and design-thinking hackathon (British University Vietnam / Turing Scheme, Summer 2026).
**[View the interactive prototype](https://www.figma.com/make/xILByDIKR2Kle86Yq8wdGG/Cultural-Integration-Tourism-App?t=LF1KMLfdZSmZV6ll-20&fullscreen=1)** (opens in Figma Make)
**Tool:** Figma Make | **Type:** Team hackathon project

## The idea

Năm helps visitors travel more sustainably and connect with local people, businesses and culture. It takes ideas from apps people already use, such as maps, language learning and gamified challenges, and applies them to responsible tourism.

## Problems it targets

- Saved social media locations that no longer exist waste journeys.
- Monolingual signage and tours limit understanding for visitors.
- Tourism brings revenue but few lasting links between visitors and local people.
- Family-run businesses and artisans are hard to find online.

## Key features

- **Live local map:** community-verified pins for local restaurants, workshops and cultural spots.
- **Audio tours:** multilingual tours narrated by local storytellers.
- **Buddy match:** visitors connect with a local before they arrive.
- **Năm Challenge:** five small sustainability tasks each day, such as buying from a local vendor or attending a craft demo.
- **Language lessons:** short Vietnamese lessons built into the daily challenge.
- **Mascot:** Dũng the water buffalo guides users through the app.
